Here's My Plan
The Aesthetic Direction
The subject is movies + anime — two worlds that share a visual language: cinematic darkness, dramatic lighting, bold title cards, and glowing accent colours you'd see on a streaming platform or an anime poster. The audience is probably young, tech-savvy, pop-culture-loving people.
So the design will feel like a dark cinema + anime fusion — not a generic dark-mode SaaS app, but something that feels like it belongs in this world.

The Signature Element
A animated glowing dual-orb background in the hero — one pink, one purple — that slowly pulse and drift. It evokes cinema projector light and anime energy at the same time. Kept subtle so it doesn't feel gimmicky.
